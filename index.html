Personal Assistant

A private tool used by one person to manage their own email, calendar, files and health data.

What it does

  • Email: reads, sorts and labels the owner's Gmail, and sends replies the owner asks for.
  • Calendar: creates and updates events in the owner's Google Calendar.
  • Files: reads documents from the owner's Google Drive so they can be filed into the owner's notes.
  • Health: copies the owner's sleep, heart rate, activity and related data from the Google Health API into their private notes, and adds meal entries the owner logs.

Who it is for

This is a personal-use app. It is not offered to the public, has no sign-up, and is used only by its developer with their own Google accounts.

Privacy

The app keeps data on the owner's computer and never sells or shares it. Read the full privacy policy.
