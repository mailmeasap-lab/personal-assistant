Privacy Policy

Personal Assistant. Effective 6 October 2026.

Who uses this app

Personal Assistant is a personal-use tool. Its only user is its developer, who runs it on their own computer with their own Google accounts. It is not available to the public.

Data the app accesses

With the user's consent, the app accesses the following Google data:

  • Gmail: reads, searches and labels messages, and sends emails and replies the user asks it to send.
  • Google Calendar: reads, creates and updates calendar events.
  • Google Drive: reads and downloads files. It does not change or delete Drive files.
  • Google Health API: reads activity and fitness data (steps, workouts, distance, active minutes), sleep data (sessions and stages), health metrics (heart rate, heart rate variability, oxygen saturation, respiratory rate, temperature, weight), nutrition and water logs, ECG readings, irregular rhythm notifications, GPS location recorded during exercise, and Google Health profile and settings. It also writes meal entries the user logs.

How the data is used

The data is used only to help the user with their own tasks: sorting and answering their email, scheduling their appointments, filing their documents, and keeping a personal record of their health in their private notes. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or any other purpose.

Where the data is stored

Notes, downloaded files and access tokens are stored only on the user's own computer. The app has no server of its own.

The user works with the app through an AI assistant. When the user asks for help with a task, the data needed for that task is sent to the AI assistant's provider to complete it. This happens only at the user's direction.

Sharing

The data is not sold, rented, or shared with anyone for their own use. It is not used to train AI models.

Personal Assistant's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and data

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the local notes, files and token files removes all data the app has stored.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are published on this page with a new effective date. Questions can be raised as an issue at github.com/mailmeasap-lab/personal-assistant.
